I love building things — products, services, projects — that are useful in the real world. I enjoy finding gaps in the market and developing around my own needs and intuition. For me, this is creative work and it recharges me. This goes way beyond coding; it's about selling, explaining the value to the outside world, and sharing what you've built.

My career over the past decade has been mostly dedicated to infrastructure automation and scaling distributed architectures. I'm now exploring, like much of the world, the frontier of AI — naturally leaning towards DevEx and agentic automation.

I'm deeply passionate about open source. I believe it carries inherent power. Beyond the code, open source means converging standards and protocols. As the cost of code collapses thanks to LLMs, reaching consensus across projects, protocols, and systems integration will become more critical than ever. This is why I've been a CNCF Ambassador since 2018, an AWS Container Hero since 2020, and currently serve on the Linux Foundation Europe Advisory Board.

Community isn't a side project for me. I helped build the Italian cloud-native scene over the years, and one of my goals now is to raise the bar by connecting and empowering technical communities across Europe.

Originally from Milan, I studied in Russia at Tomsk Polytechnic University and lived in London. I'm married with two kids, and I'm now based in Amsterdam.
