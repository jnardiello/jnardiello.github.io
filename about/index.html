About

Software engineer. Founder. Infrastructure — from bare metal to Kubernetes.

Story

I'm a software engineer who spent the last decade working on infrastructure automation — a deep-tech founder who turns hard technology into products.

In 2016 I bootstrapped SIGHUP, where we created one of the most popular independent Kubernetes distributions — still used across the industry, and the only low-level distribution created and maintained entirely in Europe.

In 2025 I moved on from SIGHUP and joined ING as Lead for Infrastructure Transformation. Building and running SIGHUP for nearly ten years was a life-long lesson, working alongside some of the most talented people I've met in my career.
